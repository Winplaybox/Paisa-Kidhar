Delete your Paisa Kidhar? data

In the app (instant)

  1. Open Paisa Kidhar? → Settings
  2. Tap Saara data delete karo → confirm

All your expenses and budget settings are permanently deleted immediately.

By email

Email support@winplaybox.in from the Google account you signed in with, subject “Delete Paisa Kidhar account”. We delete your data and sign-in record within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Profile (income, fixed costs, preferences) and every expense entry. Nothing is retained, except anonymous server logs kept up to 30 days for security.
