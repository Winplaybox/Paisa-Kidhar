Privacy Policy — Paisa Kidhar?

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Publisher: WinPlayBox, Mumbai, India · Contact: support@winplaybox.in

What we collect

  • Account: your Google account email, name and a unique user ID, used only to sign you in.
  • Budget data you enter: monthly income, fixed costs, and expenses (amount, merchant, category, date, optional note).
  • Imports: bank SMS you paste are parsed on your phone; only the extracted amount, merchant, category and date are saved. Statement files (PDF/Excel) are parsed in memory on our server and are never stored.
  • Ads: Google AdMob may collect the Advertising ID, device and interaction data to show and measure ads, subject to your consent choices.

We do not read your SMS inbox, contacts, location or bank credentials.

How it is stored

Data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security — only your signed-in account can read or change your rows. All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

Sharing

We never sell your data. We share data only with processors needed to run the app: Supabase (database & auth), Render (statement parsing API) and Google (sign-in, AdMob).

Your choices

  • Export all your expenses anytime: Settings → Export.
  • Delete all your data anytime: Settings → “Saara data delete karo”, or email us (see account deletion).
  • Change ad consent: Settings → Ad privacy options (where applicable).

Children

The app is intended for users 18 and older.

Changes

We will update this page and the date above if this policy changes.
